Don't Tap Red Privacy Policy

Developed by Chivlev Games, a game development unit of Chivlev Studio.

Effective Date: 17 October 2026
Last Updated: 17 October 2026

Don't Tap Red (the “Game”) is developed and published by Chivlev Games, a game development unit of Chivlev Studio. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Don't Tap Red.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Don't Tap Red does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, address, or other personal details to play. The game does not intentionally request these details through its gameplay.

2. Gameplay Data and Local Storage

To support game functionality, Don't Tap Red may save game-related information on your device, such as high scores, gameplay progress, and preferences. This information is stored locally using device application storage and is not intentionally sent to Chivlev Studio through the game's gameplay functions.

Depending on your device and its operating-system settings, locally stored application data may be included in device backup or restore services operated by your platform provider. Such services are subject to the provider's policies and settings.

3. Audio and Device Features

The Game uses locally bundled audio files to play gameplay sound effects. Playing these sounds does not require access to your microphone or audio recordings.

4. Internet, Advertising and Analytics

The current version is designed for offline gameplay and does not intentionally transmit gameplay scores or progress to a Chivlev Studio server. It does not currently integrate third-party advertising or dedicated analytics SDKs.

If future versions introduce online features, advertising, analytics, or additional data processing, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

5. Third-Party Services and Platform Providers

The Game runs on Android and may be installed or updated through Google Play. Google and your device or operating-system provider may process information independently in connection with app downloads, updates, security, diagnostics, backups, or store services under their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy describes the Game's own practices, not all processing performed independently by those providers.

6. Data Sharing

Don't Tap Red does not intentionally share locally stored gameplay information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Information you voluntarily send to us through a separate communication channel, such as email, will be handled as described below.

7. Support and Feedback

If you choose to email us, we may receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information to respond to questions, investigate reported problems, and consider feedback. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data saved locally remains on your device until it is removed through applicable in-app or device controls, app-data clearing, or uninstalling the Game, subject to any operating-system backup or restore settings. You can contact us about information you have voluntarily provided by email and request its deletion, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Don't Tap Red does not require personal information to play and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

10. Data Security

We design the Game to minimize the collection and transmission of user information. No method of electronic storage or communication is guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in support communications.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its functionality, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy webpage with a revised “Last Updated” date.
